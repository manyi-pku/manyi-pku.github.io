三句 opening

I work on low-Reynolds-number microswimmer hydrodynamics, especially self-diffusiophoretic particles and swimmers in complex fluids. My talk is about how spheroidal geometry changes viscoelastic corrections to phoretic propulsion.
I am also interested in how boundaries and complex fluids modify microswimmer trajectories, including near-wall squirmers and bacterial-style swimmers.
I have a separate project on reinforcement learning for microswimmer navigation, so I am interested in how action-space design and physical constraints affect learned control policies.
